# Buildathon Demo Script

Target duration: Approximately 90 seconds.

## Opening

Problem:

Myanmar online sellers manage customer chats, orders, payment screenshots, and financial records across disconnected tools.

Our product helps turn those scattered interactions into structured business records.

## Step 1 — Messenger

Show a real Messenger browser window containing a synthetic customer conversation.

## Step 2 — Floating Dolphin

Open the desktop dolphin assistant over Messenger.

## Step 3 — Chat to Order

Paste the conversation.

Show AI extracting products, quantities, and customer information.

## Step 4 — Review and Confirm

Edit a quantity.

Confirm the draft order.

Show the successful saved-order state.

## Step 5 — Voucher

Generate the voucher and display its preview.

## Step 6 — Payment Matching

Upload a synthetic transaction screenshot.

Show extracted fields and a candidate order match.

Explain that the screenshot itself does not verify money received.

Demonstrate seller verification using the prepared trusted demo record.

## Step 7 — Financial Dashboard

Open the website.

Show the saved order, confirmed payment, and updated dashboard.

Emphasize that sales, cash received, expenses, and profit are different concepts.

## Closing Message

From Chat to Order, From Order to Profit.

A friendly AI assistant designed for Myanmar online sellers.

## Fallback

If live backend or AI is unavailable, use a clearly identified local demonstration mode with synthetic data.

If Messenger is unavailable, use the prepared synthetic chat webpage.

Never represent a simulated result as a live production transaction.
